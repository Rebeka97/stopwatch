<h2>Features & Capabilities</h2>
- Precise Time Tracking: Real-time measurement down to milliseconds (Minutes : Seconds : Milliseconds).
<br>
- Core Controls: Intuitive Start, Pause, Reset, and Lap actions to manage your timing sessions seamlessly.
<br>
- Lap Management: Detailed history tracking for all recorded laps, complete with individual split times and a quick-clear trash option.
<br>
- Custom Themes: Personalize your timing experience with multiple pre-built aesthetic themes including Pink (Default), B&W, Autumn, Nature, Mermaid, and Galaxy.
<br>
- Modern Neumorphic UI: Soft, tactile design elements that mimic physical buttons and cards for a clean, premium look.

<h2>Theme Options</h2>
- Pink: Soft, warm rose tones for a cozy, charming vibe.
<br>
- B&W: Minimalist high-contrast black and white styling.
<br>
- Autumn: Earthy, rich seasonal shades.
<br>
- Nature: Calming organic greens and soft accents.
<br>
- Mermaid: Magical iridescent, bubbly, and dreamy aquatic background aesthetic with crystal-clear glassmorphism elements.
<br>
- Galaxy: Cosmic, dark atmospheric styling.
<br>
- Home: Sophisticated sunlit textured backdrop paired with a clean, frosted glassmorphism stopwatch card.

<h2>Tech Stack</h2>
Framework: Flutter (Dart)
<br>
UI Pattern: Neumorphic design system with dynamic theme switching
